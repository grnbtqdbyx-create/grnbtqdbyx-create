## 2. How the syndication endpoints work (Yahoo Finance, Barchart, FinancialContent, AP News, Business Insider, USA Today, NewsBreak)

### Takeaway
None of the named outlets is bought directly. They are automatic feed endpoints of the wholesale wire. Newsfile and MarketersMedia push each release into the press-release feeds of FinancialContent, Barchart, Yahoo Finance, The Globe and Mail, Digital Journal and others. FinancialContent's white-label pages also put the same story on dozens of local-broadcaster and newspaper sites. NewsBreak gets it through publisher accounts such as "DMR News." This is how "500+ outlets" is reached with one submission.

### Cited Findings
- FinancialContent hosts wire releases under slugs that name the wire (`/article/marketersmedia-YYYY-M-D-…`, `/article/newsfile-YYYY-M-D-…`). The same article appears on multiple white-label partner subdomains (e.g. `/stocks/`, `/worldnow/`, `/clarkebroadcasting.mymotherlode/`) — [FinancialContent marketersmedia](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/marketersmedia-2025-11-6-mediaboost-launches-fast-track-media-exposure-platform-for-startups-seeking-ai-indexed-credibility), [worldnow example](https://markets.financialcontent.com/worldnow/article/marketersmedia-2024-10-29-green-springs-capital-group-llc-announces-formation-of-cg-construction-group-llc), [FinancialContent newsfile](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/newsfile-2026-2-23-genviral-releases-openclaw-skill-to-automate-social-media-content-across-six-platforms).
