The founder name on TrustMRR is almost certainly **not** the real operator. It was pulled from the X handle @ricardo, and that account belongs to an unrelated Portuguese writer and filmmaker named Ricardo Pinho. Roamify's public CEO and founder is **Dveer Zvi**.

## 3. The likely brand (Roamify): product, eSIM pricing, VPN, supplier, affiliates, marketing

### Takeaway
Roamify is a low-price travel eSIM seller with 6,311 plans in 276 destinations, from $0.67 per plan and from $1.17 per unlimited day. It sells on the web and in apps, and adds a "super-app" layer: calling, SMS, insurance, VPN, AI planner, cashback, store credit and referrals.

It grows through heavy multilingual SEO and AI-search (GEO) content, affiliates and partners, and community deal channels. The VPN appears only as a feature tile. No public VPN pricing, provider or product page was found.

### Takeaway

## 5. Lessons for inboxesim.com (web eSIM SaaS adding a web-only VPN)

### Takeaway
The useful lessons are not about the VPN itself, which is barely marketed here. They are about selling a one-off travel product at large scale on the web:

- extreme entry pricing
- programmatic multilingual destination and plan pages
- AI and LLM-readable catalogue files
- affiliate tracking that cannot be turned off
- referral credit plus a stored balance
- a "travel essentials" super-account that cross-sells add-ons at checkout

A VPN fits best as a per-trip add-on bundled with the eSIM, following the Stellar model, not as a separately marketed subscription.

### Cited Findings (tactics observed)
- **Loss-leader entry prices:** Roamify headlines "from $0.67" and "unlimited 1-day plans at $1.17", against AT&T/Verizon at $12 a day. — [getroamify.com](https://www.getroamify.com/)
- **Programmatic SEO across 54 locales:** One page per country and one per plan, with Markdown twins and llms-full.txt listing every plan's price. — [llms.txt](https://www.getroamify.com/llms.txt)
- **Data-driven "research" pages as link bait:** A price index, cheapest destinations and unlimited destinations, with anchored statistics and refreshed daily. — [llms.txt](https://www.getroamify.com/llms.txt)
- **Bundled feature grid of upsells:** Calling, SMS, Insurance, VPN, AI Planner, Cashback, Referral and Roam Credit. — [getroamify.com](https://www.getroamify.com/)
- **Referral program:** $3 for the referrer and $3 for the referee. Stored balance ("Roam Credit") with gift cards. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/roamify-travel-esim-data-call/id6472396976), [pricing.md](https://www.getroamify.com/pricing.md)
- **Payment options:** Card and wallet, PayPal and Coinbase crypto at checkout. — [pricing.md](https://www.getroamify.com/pricing.md)
- **Affiliate reviewers:** Affiliate tracking always runs. Roamify shows an award from an affiliate review site, with the affiliation disclosed. — [getroamify.com](https://www.getroamify.com/)
- **Community deal channels:** WhatsApp and Telegram, with promo codes such as BLACKFRIDAY. — [getroamify.com](https://www.getroamify.com/), [pricing.md](https://www.getroamify.com/pricing.md)
- **B2B and API white-label as a second engine:** Partners include airlines, banks and MNOs. — [press release](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/roamify-achieves-record-scale-20-194500920.html)
- **Competitor VPN bundling:** Stellar bundles its VPN free for the plan's duration and sells it at €9.99/month on its own. — [MyBestSim](https://mybestsim.com/en/best-esim-with-vpn/)

### Inferences (recommendations; not verified outcomes)
- **VPN pass at checkout:** Offer a "trip VPN pass" as a checkout add-on, priced per day or per week to match the eSIM's validity (for example $1–3 per trip). Consider making it free on higher tiers. This mirrors Stellar and fits a one-off purchase model with no app.
- **Destination VPN pages:** Build web-only pages for each destination where VPN need is real: China, UAE, Turkey, Russia, plus streaming abroad. Roamify's China and streaming posts already rank on these intents.
- **Copy the AI-file pattern:** Publish llms.txt, Markdown versions of each plan and a daily price index. It is cheap to build and helps citations in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers.
- **Store credit:** Add credit or wallet plus referral credit to lift repeat purchases, which are the main lever in a no-MRR business.
- **Watch the seasonality:** About 35% swing from trough to peak. Plan ad spend and cash flow around it.
- **Read TrustMRR founder fields with care:** These fields can be wrong. The @ricardo mapping shows that founder names and follower counts on stealth listings can be misattributed.

### Gaps
- No public conversion, attach-rate or AOV data for VPN add-ons in eSIM checkouts.
- The best-performing marketing channel for the likely brand is unknown, because traffic integrations are not connected on TrustMRR.
