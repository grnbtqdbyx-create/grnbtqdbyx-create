# How small/indie VPN businesses are built and sourced (wholesale/white-label vs own servers): stacks, costs, margins

Research date: 2026-11-12. Most Flippa listings below have **ended**. Flippa shows "Ended N days ago" rather than a date, so approximate end dates are given as (2026-11-12 minus N days). All Flippa financials are **seller-reported** unless a "Verified/Vetted" badge or connected integration is noted. Even with a badge, Flippa's vetting text is generic boilerplate. Where revenue is "implied", I calculated it from profit and margin; the listing does not state it.

## Q1. Marketplace listings: what do sellers disclose about stack, provider, costs and revenue?

### Takeaway
Small VPN listings on Flippa fall into four groups: (a) web-only resellers of a larger provider's accounts (~$1–2 per active user per month wholesale, 60–92% stated margins, tiny MRR of $300–2.5k); (b) "complete" brands with their own apps that rent capacity from a larger VPN provider; (c) ad-funded free Android/iOS VPN apps with their own cheap servers and large install bases, which report the largest profits ($1k–55k/mo); and (d) older subscription networks on rented dedicated servers. Sellers almost never name the upstream provider or server host.

### Inferences
- **Two distinct worlds.** (1) Paid-subscription brands built on resold capacity (BeetVPN, FullVPN, multivpn.org) report high gross margins (49–92%) but tiny MRR, from a few hundred dollars to about $2.5k, and sell for $5k–20k. (2) Ad-funded free VPN apps on Android with their own cheap servers (VPN Fast, Ryn VPN) report the only large profits ($33k–55k/mo). Their value comes from installs and AdMob, not subscribers.
- **Server cost is small next to revenue for sellers who disclose it.** Examples: Simple VPN's $28/mo, Ryn's "server maintenance" at ≈14% of implied revenue, and the 2013-era network's ≈$3k/mo costs on $9k revenue. The binding cost is user acquisition plus store or payment fees, not servers.
- **Sellers rarely name the upstream provider.** "A larger VPN provider" (FullVPN) and "$1 per active user" (BeetVPN) are consistent with wholesale programs like VPNresellers ($1.99, then $0.99 per active account; see Q3), but no listing confirms the vendor.
- The largest subscriber-based listing (20k paying members, ~$45k/mo implied revenue) runs "proprietary client and server software" at only a 29% margin. At scale, an owned network plus support and marketing is costly.
- The "Verified" badge mostly reflects connected integrations (Stripe/GA/AdMob/PayPal). Margins that don't reconcile (BeetVPN, Simple VPN) show seller figures need checking.

### Gaps
- Most listing end dates are approximate. Flippa also hides full P&L, subscriber counts and supplier names behind NDA or sign-up.
- I could not retrieve a current (live in Nov 2026) VPN listing on Flippa. All listings found are ended or sold. **Acquire.com, Microns.io and Empire Flippers were not searched** because of the tool-call budget. Treat this as an open gap.
- No listing names its white-label vendor or VPS host (Hetzner, OVH, DigitalOcean, etc.).
- The #11730407 and #10885839 descriptions were truncated, so no stack details were obtained.

## Q2. Founder write-ups and the "weekly-subscription mobile VPN app factory"

### Takeaway
Credible first-person indie VPN P&Ls are rare. Most "how to start a VPN business" content is written by resellers or vendors and has an affiliate or commercial interest. The visible founder accounts are small (a Telegram-bot VPN with 8 paying users; a VPNresellers-based blog business with "few clients"). The high-revenue mobile VPN segment is dominated by weekly-priced paywalls ($9.99–14.99/week), often run by opaque app families on shared infrastructure, some with documented scam or fake-review tactics. I found no public CAC/ROAS figures specific to VPN.

### Cited Findings
- **VPN-Time (Habr, translated; year not shown on page):** a Telegram-bot VPN on OpenVPN with servers in the Netherlands and Luxembourg costing 2,500 RUB/month. Spend: ~13,000 RUB on design and ~15,000 RUB on ads. Result: 8 paying users (2 from ads), 1,350 RUB revenue. About 80% of ad visitors never connected. Word of mouth beat ads. Break-even estimated at 100 user-hours/day — [Habr](https://habr.com/en/articles/990240)
- **IM Rhys reseller article (pub. 2021-01-06, upd. 2023-11-02):** the author runs a VPNresellers-based brand at "$1.99 per user per month" billed daily, $0.99 after 1,000 active accounts, $25 minimum deposit. He had "few clients", gave no actual revenue, and pitched a hypothetical "$6,000 per month" from 1–2k members. He tried his own servers and said "the monthly running costs of the servers can be very high" at small scale. No explicit affiliate disclosure on the page; links use "/go/" redirects — [imrhys.com](https://www.imrhys.com/start-a-vpn-business/)
- **StringVPN (Apr 2021):** charged $9.99/week, estimated at ~$1M/month by Kosta Eleftheriou (method not described). It used "Recommended by Apple" Safari pop-ups and fake translated reviews — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2021/04/07/scam-ios-apps-latest-a-vpn-that-charges-9-99-week-uses-recommended-by-apple-pop-ups/)
- Older reporting (circa 2017) has developer Johnny Lin documenting a VPN app pushing a $99.99/week subscription during a "free trial" setup, with Apple Search Ads abused to look like organic results — [Threatpost](https://threatpost.com/abuse-of-apple-search-ads-feature-leading-to-fraud/126265/) (via search snippet)
- **VeePN on iOS (Adapty paywall library, info updated 2026-11-07):** weekly plans at $9.99, $11.99 and $14.99; annual at $69.99. Estimated revenue is $100k/mo in the header and $200k/mo in the About section, which conflict. These are third-party estimates — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/vpn-service/)
- Adapty also lists a "Free VPN" paywall at an estimated ~$300k/month (estimate, via search snippet) — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/free-vpn/)
- **Apple Ads benchmarks (Adapty 2026 report, 2025 data, 8,000+ apps):** global median CPI $0.51, spend-weighted average $1.34, US CPI $2.51, US CPT ~$1.58. Install-to-trial is 3.66% on Apple Ads versus 1.45% on other paid channels. **No VPN-specific figures are public**; the full report is gated — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/apple-ads-for-subscription-apps/)
- Appfigures (Aug 2023) estimated that VPN apps across the App Store and Google Play earned over $20M net in a month, and noted Nord spending on Apple Search Ads (via search snippet) — [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20230804?f=2)
- **App families on shared infrastructure ("Hidden Links", FOCI/PoPETs 2025):** three families of Android VPN apps with 700M+ combined Google Play downloads secretly share VPN servers and credentials, including hard-coded Shadowsocks passwords. Family B (Global VPN, XY VPN, Super Z VPN…) has all servers hosted by one company, GlobalTeleHost Corp. Tech Transparency Project had earlier linked some of these providers to Qihoo 360 — [PETS/FOCI 2025](https://www.petsymposium.org/foci/2025/foci-2025-0008.php); [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2025/08/19/android-vpn-apps-used-by-millions-are-covertly-connected-and-insecure/); [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2025/09/popular-android-vpn-apps-found-to-have-security-flaws-and-china-links)
- **Templates for the app factory:** CodeCanyon VPN templates ship the app and admin panel but **not servers**. Axe VPN (Flutter + Laravel admin, OpenVPN/WireGuard) says "VPN servers are not included… Buyers may use any compatible third-party VPN server provider". Android OVPN Client's admin panel configures your own VPS servers. Nerd VPN is Flutter + IAP + admin panel. Separately, "Gate VPN" (on Product Hunt) advertises automatic **VPNGate** sync with OpenVPN and a PHP/MySQL panel. These come from search snippets; I did not open the listing pages — [CodeCanyon Axe VPN](https://codecanyon.net/item/axe-vpn-flutter-app-with-admin-panel-linkze/59131048); [CodeCanyon Android OVPN Client](https://codecanyon.net/item/android-ovpn-client/28482186); [CodeCanyon Nerd VPN](https://codecanyon.net/item/nerd-vpn-flutter-vpn-android-full-application-with-iap-integrated-with-backend-and-admin-panel/28722345); [Product Hunt Gate VPN](https://www.producthunt.com/products/gate-vpn-professional-vpn-app-sale)
- Fiverr gigs sell "VPN app + OpenVPN/V2Ray/WireGuard + server + admin panel" builds, which shows the commoditised supply chain for the app factory — [Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/hassanjutt239/develop-vpn-app-for-android-ios-openvpn-v2ray-wireguard)
- **Self-hosted server costs (2026):** a Hetzner entry VPS runs ~€4.50–5.49/mo with 20 TB of EU egress included and ~€1/TB after; Hetzner announced price increases effective 1 April 2026 — [devrunners](https://devrunners.com/blog/self-hosted-vpn-vs-commercial-vpn); [VPNSmith](https://www.vpnsmith.com/en/blog/best-vps-for-vpn-2026). Hetzner says its Cost Optimized plan is adequate for ~30 simultaneous users — [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com/blog/your-own-vpn-server-with-wireguard-what-are-the-benefits/)

### Inferences
- The mobile app factory model (template or Fiverr app + own cheap VPS or shared family servers + weekly paywall + paid UA) is where the large revenue sits. Server cost per user is negligible on €5 VPS boxes with 20 TB of egress, so economics are driven by CPI × install-to-paid conversion versus weekly ARPU, minus Apple/Google's 15–30%.
- Paid acquisition is hard for small indie brands. VPN-Time's 2 paying users from 15k RUB in ads and the observation that "most users who need a VPN have already found 'their' VPN" both point that way. Organic ASO and installs (Ryn VPN "acquired organically", AironVPN's organic downloads) explain most successful small listings.
- Weekly pricing at $9.99+ is associated with both legitimate large players (VeePN) and scam apps. Apple's enforcement history is a platform risk for any new weekly-paywall VPN.

### Gaps
- I found **no Reddit, IndieHackers or X founder post** with a verifiable VPN P&L, CAC or ROAS in this session. Searches returned only vendor content.
- No VPN-specific CPI/CAC/ROAS benchmarks are public; the Adapty report gates category data. TikTok UGC, Superwall and RevenueCat use in VPN apps was not evidenced by any source I found.
- Adapty revenue figures are third-party estimates with internal inconsistencies.

## Q3. White-label/wholesale providers: pricing, terms and reputations

### Takeaway
VPNresellers is the only provider with fully transparent public per-user pricing: $1.99 per active account per month, falling to $0.99 at 1k accounts and $0.79 at 250k, with no setup fee and a $25 minimum credit. It matches the ~$1–2 per active user costs in the small listings. PureWL (PureVPN), VPNGN, Windscribe WL and Pango/Aura's Hydra SDK sell via sales calls, with reported floors around $250–1,000/month. Independent reviews of these white-label programs are essentially non-existent.

### Cited Findings
- **VPNresellers (page © 2026):** 1–999 active accounts at $1.99/mo; 1,000–24,999 at $0.99; tiers down to $0.79 at 250k–499k; custom pricing at 500k+. One rate applies to the whole base. Billed daily from credit, no setup, signup fee or minimums, $25 minimum top-up. Custom branding costs $39/mo (needs a $500 credit balance; not on iOS). Brandless apps are included. The vendor's example: 250 accounts at $9.99 retail gives $2,498 revenue, $498 wholesale and $2,000 profit (80.1%) — [VPNresellers](https://www.vpnresellers.com/white-label-vpn-cost)
- **PureWL:** the most consistent public signal is a $250/month lowest paid plan (pricing snapshot 2026-05-06); custom plans above. Aggregators conflict ($1.99/active user on SaaSWorthy next to a $1,000/mo plan; Capterra "$1000 per user" looks like an error) — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/purewl/pricing); [SaaSWorthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/purewl/pricing); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10017793/PureWL/). PureWL's own example assumes a $1,000/mo plan at $5/mo retail, breaking even at ~250 users — [PureWL](https://www.purewl.com/white-label-vpn-business/)
- PureVPN content says many resellers spend 10–20% of monthly revenue on support (vendor source) — [PureVPN](https://www.purevpn.com/white-label/white-label-vpn-tco-calculator/)
- Vendors' break-even claims vary: one says 200–500 active subscribers for hosted white label, another 750–1,000 (vendor marketing, unverified) — [vpncrafter](https://vpncrafter.com/how-to-build-a-profitable-vpn-business/); [PureWL](https://www.purewl.com/what-is-a-white-label-vpn/)
- **VPNGN:** SourceForge lists $250/mo with a trial. Software Advice says white label is offered only to "selected partners" who drive significant daily sales. Zero user reviews found — [SourceForge](https://www.sourceforge.net/software/product/VPNGN); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/vpn/vpngn-profile)
- **Windscribe white label:** a vendor blog claims servers in 69+ countries and launch "in weeks". No public pricing; strategy-call only — [Windscribe](https://windscribe.com/blog/windscribe-white-label-vpn/)
- **Pango / Aura Partner SDK:** SDKs support IPSec, OpenVPN and the proprietary Hydra protocol, with client libraries for iOS, macOS, Windows and Android. No public pricing; "talk with an expert" — [Pango docs](https://pango.gitbook.io/paas/sdk/unified-sdk-for-windows); [Aura docs](https://auravpn.gitbook.io/paas/sdk/unified-vpn-sdk-for-apple); [Pango](https://www.pango.co/vpn)
- Reseller risk noted by a reseller guide: don't sell lifetime plans, because if VPNresellers disappears or removes the service, clients lose access — [fundz / reseller guide](https://www.fundz.net/sales-intelligence-blog/vpn-reseller-program-2026-guide) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- **Typical cost structure for a small paid-VPN brand** (synthesised from the sources above; illustrative, not audited):
  - Wholesale capacity: ~$0.80–2.00 per active user per month (VPNresellers tiers; BeetVPN's $1/active user), or flat $250–1,000/mo plans (PureWL/VPNGN).
  - Own VPS servers: ~€5/mo per box handling tens of concurrent users with 20 TB egress, so well under $0.50 per active user at moderate utilisation. Owning servers mainly adds ops, abuse/DMCA handling and support time, not much hard cost.
  - Platform fees: Apple/Google take 15% (small-business program, or year-two subscriptions) to 30%. I have no direct source in these notes, so treat these figures as standard store policy. Web: Stripe/PayPal at ~3% + fixed fee (BeetVPN uses Stripe; not separately sourced).
  - Support: 10–20% of revenue per a vendor estimate.
  - Paid acquisition: the dominant cost for any growth-stage app, but no VPN-specific CAC is public. US Apple Ads CPI ≈ $2.51 (all categories, 2025).
- **Answer to "wholesaler + markup or own servers?":** both exist, and which one depends on segment.
  - Web/desktop subscription micro-brands, which are the most common small Flippa listings, mostly **buy wholesale** ("larger VPN provider", "$1/active user", "$2 accounts resold at $6") and keep 50–90% gross margin on tiny volumes.
  - Ad-funded and weekly-paywall mobile apps mostly **run their own cheap servers** or share a family's backend (VPN Fast, Ryn VPN, GamersVPN, Simple VPN, Hidden Links families), because at millions of installs, per-user wholesale fees would destroy margin.
  - Long-lived subscription networks (2013-era $9k/mo; 20k-member brand) run **own dedicated/virtual servers** and proprietary clients.

### Gaps
- No public per-user pricing for PureWL, Windscribe WL, VPNGN or Pango/Aura. Independent reviews of these white-label programs were not found.
- I did not verify the reputations of providers asked about ("ZeroVPN", "Gecko"). These names did not surface in searches and may not exist as white-label vendors.
- No source gave an explicit app-store fee or payment-processor fee figure in this session. The 15–30% store fee is standard platform policy but is not cited from a primary page here.
