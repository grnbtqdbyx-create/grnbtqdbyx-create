# Revenue Benchmarks: Small/Indie VPN, Top-up / Airtime / Gift Card, and eSIM Businesses

Research date: 2026-11-12. All TrustMRR figures were pulled directly from trustmrr.com startup pages on 2026-11-12 (page HTML fetched; "last 30 days" revenue and MRR are as displayed that day). "Verified" = revenue pulled by TrustMRR from the payment provider API (Stripe, RevenueCat, Superwall, DodoPayments, Polar). Marketplace (Flippa / Microns / Acquire) numbers are seller-reported unless noted.

Method note on TrustMRR: the site search (`/search?q=vpn`) is rendered client-side and returned no listings to a fetcher. Workaround used: downloaded `trustmrr.com/startup-sitemap.xml` + `sitemap-0/1.xml` (12,445 startup slugs), grepped slugs for vpn / proxy / privacy / tunnel / esim / sim / gift / topup / recharge / airtime / voucher / prepaid / card, then fetched each candidate page. Also scanned the first page (30 startups each) of the security, travel, utilities, fintech, ecommerce and mobile-apps category pages. Limitation: startups whose slug does not contain a keyword (brand names) could be missed.

## TrustMRR (Stripe/RevenueCat-verified) listings: VPN, proxy, privacy, eSIM, telecom, gift card, top-up

### Takeaway
TrustMRR has a handful of verified VPN businesses (BambooVPN ~$9.8k MRR, BlueVPN ~$2.3k MRR, an IPTV+VPN app bundle ~$11.7k MRR) and a cluster of 10+ tiny travel-eSIM resellers that mostly earn $400–$4,500/month in one-time sales; proxy sellers are the outliers (HypeProxies ~$239k/30 days). No airtime/mobile top-up reseller was found on TrustMRR, and the only gift-card business earns ~$274/30 days.

### Inferences
- A realistic verified range for a small indie VPN app with paid acquisition/ASO is ~$2k–$12k MRR after 1–4 years; the BambooVPN case shows ~$10k MRR is reachable in ~14 months with weekly subscriptions ($3.99/wk).
- Travel-eSIM resellers on TrustMRR cluster at $0.4k–$4.5k/month revenue (gross, not profit), with stated margins 46–65%; i.e. typical net profit of a few hundred to ~$2.5k/month. Many are <1 year old.
- B2B "white-label platform" plays (eSIM Panel, MEGAESIM.US) are small too ($1–2.4k/month), suggesting the white-label SaaS layer is not automatically more lucrative than retail.
- Absence of top-up/airtime businesses on TrustMRR likely reflects that these are low-margin transaction businesses often paid outside Stripe (local payment rails), not that none exist — but there is no verified indie benchmark for them.

### Gaps
- TrustMRR's own search UI could not be fetched (client-side rendering); brand-named VPN/top-up startups not matching slug keywords may be missing.
- IndieHackers product pages and Open Startups lists were not checked in depth (no VPN/top-up results surfaced in search); Starter Story had no VPN interview surfaced.

### Inferences
- Flippa "Profit Multiple" figures on VPN listings reconcile with annual profit (e.g., $20,000 / ($623×12) = 2.7x), so small VPNs trade around 2–4x annual profit — consistent with Flippa's 2.4–2.7x Apps/SaaS averages; tiny deals (<$100k) skew lower (~1.8x).
- VPN businesses with high revenue but low margin (29% for the 20k-member VPN) trade at ~0.5x revenue; service/reseller-type businesses (which top-up resellers resemble) carry the lowest multiples (Flippa Service avg 1.2x).
- TrustMRR asking prices (6.8x ARR for BambooVPN) are seller asks, not closed deals, and are well above Flippa closing norms.

### Gaps
- No Flippa/Acquire/Microns listing for a mobile top-up / airtime reselling site with financials was found.
- Flippa's closed sale prices (vs asking) are mostly hidden behind login.
- Empire Flippers and SideProjectors listings were not directly checked.

## Larger reference players: VPN apps, top-up/airtime platforms, gift cards, eSIM

### Takeaway
Top-up/airtime is a high-turnover, thin-margin business: Ding (Ezetop) turned over ~$501M in 2024 but made ~$15.3M after-tax profit (~3%). eSIM leaders are far larger (Holafly ~$200M revenue in 2024; Airalo reported $150M in 2023). Top VPN apps earn hundreds of thousands of dollars per week per market on Android alone.

### Inferences
- Top-up reselling economics: gross turnover is large but net margin is low (Ding ~3% after-tax on turnover; ~9% net revenue take in 2017; reseller discounts 6–15% per vendor marketing). A small founder doing $10k/month in top-up volume would likely net only a few hundred dollars/month before payment fees — far worse than a subscription VPN at equal revenue.
- eSIM is the best-documented "digital connectivity resale" comparable at indie scale (many TrustMRR cases), but market leaders are hundreds of millions of dollars; indie eSIM stores mostly sit under $5k/month.
